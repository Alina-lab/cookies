Авторы: Thao H.T. Nguyen, Rui Ding, Afroditi Chatzifragkou, Lisa Methven и Julia Rodriguez-Garcia. [DOI 10.17864/1947.000384](https://doi.org/10.17864/1947.000384).

(2022). *Soluble fibres as sucrose replacers: Effects on physical and sensory properties of sugar-reduced short-dough biscuits*. LWT, 167, 113837. [DOI 10.1016/j.lwt.2022.113837](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lwt.2022.113837). Опубликованная версия статьи указывает CC BY 4.0, © 2022 The Authors.
